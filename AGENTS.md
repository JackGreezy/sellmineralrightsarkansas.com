# Sell Mineral Rights Arkansas — Site-Specific Rules

This project has its own Arkansas mineral-acquisition voice and its own Kalmar-derived visual system. Never import another mineral-rights site's hero, FAQ, related rail, closing CTA, decorative language, or generic card stack.

## Voice lock

Write as an Arkansas county-record and mineral-acquisition business. Use Fayetteville Shale production evidence, Smackover mineral and brine scope, county deed records, reservation language, owner fractions, division orders, paid decimals, lease burdens, and written conveyance terms. Never use first-person business narration, guaranteed value, instant-cash language, fabricated reviews, or highest-offer claims.

## Visual lock

Preserve Kalmar's real Stabil Grotesk typography, two-tier white header, full-width carousel hero, pale-green editorial cards, lilac and plum split bands, large industrial imagery, mosaic contact page, equipment-style taxonomy indexes, and dense plum footer. Slug pages use one wide hero image, a broad readable owner brief, an Arkansas record-check FAQ, related acquisition-file navigation, and a strong closing review CTA.

## Release blockers

- The homepage hero contains one visible SEO-driven H1 and a working mapped image.
- Every taxonomy slug has exactly one image: its hero image.
- The street address may appear only inside a Google Maps embed query or non-visible metadata.
- Every footer contains one Google Maps embed and no `img`, `picture`, `svg`, `video`, `source`, or `canvas` media.
- No miniature images, blank images, one-character vertical text, squeezed desktop columns, invisible copy, weak contrast, clipped sections, or overlapping header elements.
- The desktop header includes real routes, `501-555-1042`, and a clear contact CTA. Dropdowns work on hover and focus with no pointer gap. The mobile drawer and every carousel control work.
- The contact form posts to `/api/contact`, routes to `arkansas@sellmineralrightsarkansas.com`, and exposes exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Every slug page retains the Kalmar font and visual theme, uses a wide centered reading measure, and ends with a clear contact and phone CTA.
- All visible business copy is third person or neutral business language, never individual-author narration.

Completion requires both faithful-home machine gates, full desktop/mobile visual review, child-repo isolation, production deployment, and live alias verification.
